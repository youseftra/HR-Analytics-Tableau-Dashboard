An interactive HR analytics dashboard built in **Tableau**, powered by a custom synthetic dataset generated via a **Python** script. This project visualizes and analyzes workforce headcount, performance metrics, salary structures, and hiring/termination dynamics within an organization.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is structured into two complementary views:

1. **Overview (Strategic View)**:
   * **Core KPIs**: Active headcount (~7,948), total hires (8,950), and total terminations (1,002).
   * **Demographics & Performance**: Analysis of gender distribution, age groups, education levels, and performance ratings.
   * **Salary & Geographic Analysis**: Distribution of adjusted salaries by age/gender and mapping of company branches (HQ vs. Branches).

2. **Details (Operational View)**:
   * A dynamic table allowing users to filter individual employee records by ID, role, department, location, salary, employment status (Hired / Terminated), and tenure.

     ## Data Generation & Pipeline (`generate_data.py`)

To power the visualization with realistic business logic, the dataset (8,950 records) was synthetically generated in Python (`pandas`, `numpy`, `faker`) incorporating customized constraints:

* **Hire & Termination Rules**: Strict enforcement of a minimum 6-month tenure before termination eligibility, targeting an overall attrition rate of 11.2%.
* **Demographic Realism**: Realistic minimum age thresholds applied (e.g., minimum age of 30 for Managers and 27 for PhD holders).
* **Adjusted Salary Logic**: Calculation of an `adjusted_salary` variable factoring in education multiplier, gender distribution trends, and age/experience scaling.
